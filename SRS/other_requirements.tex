This chapter contains requirements that do not fit into the previous categories.

\section{Legal Requirements}

\begin{itemize}
\item The system shall comply with open-source licensing for used libraries (SUMO, YOLO, RLlib).
\item Intellectual property rights for custom algorithms shall be retained by the development team.
\end{itemize}

\section{Installation and Deployment}

\begin{itemize}
\item The system shall provide automated installation scripts.
\item Deployment shall support containerization (Docker) for reproducibility.
\end{itemize}

\section{Data Management}

\begin{itemize}
\item Training data shall be anonymized to protect privacy.
\item Logs shall be retained for a minimum of 1 year for analysis.
\end{itemize}
